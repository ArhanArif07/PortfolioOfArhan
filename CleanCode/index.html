Process

How I work

Four steps, no mystery. You see progress as it happens, not at the end.

01

Understand

We start with the actual brief: who it’s for, what it needs to do, and how it should feel.

02

Build

Layout, code, motion and content, built in one pass so nothing gets lost between design and development.

03

Refine

Testing on real devices, tightening what feels slow, and polishing until it holds up.

04

Launch

Live on your domain with a clear handover, documented and ready to keep building.

FAQ

Straight answers

The questions people usually ask before starting a project.

What kind of websites do you build?

Business sites, landing pages and web products — mostly. If a project needs real front-end work, custom layout, motion or logic, that is the work.

Can you work from an existing design?

Send over the design and I’ll build it faithfully — or start from it and suggest changes where they’d help on smaller screens.

Can you add interactions and animations?

Yes. Motion is part of the build, not an add-on, and it is written to stay smooth on a mid-range phone rather than only on a fast laptop.

How does your process work?

Understand the brief, design what’s missing, build it, then refine on real devices. You see progress as it happens, not at the end.

Do you build responsive sites and web tools?

Everything is built mobile-first, and I’ve built interactive tools and small web products where the interface has real logic in it.

Do you work with clients outside Aligarh?

Yes. I’m based in Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh and work with clients across India, mostly remotely.
